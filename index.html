Botanica · Prana

Four builds of the same landing page.

Same words, same films, same palette. What changes is how the shop behaves and, in the last one, how much of the page answers the scroll wheel.

  • Shop band

    A brochure-style shop band pinned down the right edge, on screen at all times.

  • Pull tab

    The shop hides behind a frosted tab on the right edge. Hover or click it to open.

    Open preview

  • Floating bottle

    A floating bottle button, bottom right, opening a cart drawer.

    Open preview

  • Static option

    Nothing follows the scroll wheel: both films play by themselves and the shop shows stills.

    Open preview

Best on a desktop browser. The layout adapts down to phones, but the films and the side-by-side shop are built for a wide screen.

Each build loads two short films, so give the first screen a moment on a slow connection. The film in the Journey section is embedded from YouTube.

The “Add to cart” buttons build real links to the Botanica store, so checking out leaves the preview and lands on the live shop.
